Given precomputed advantages and log probabilities from the current policy, previous policy, and reference policy, compute the scalar PPO clipped surrogate loss. The operation combines a clipped policy ratio with a KL penalty and reduces the result across a batch of sequences.

Implementation Requirements

  • Implement solve(advantages, log_pi, log_pi_old, log_ref, output, clip_eps, beta, B, S) with the signature unchanged
  • Write the single scalar result into the preallocated output tensor
  • Use only native features of the selected framework; external libraries are not permitted
  • All input tensors have shape [B, S] and the output has shape [1]

For each batch element b and sequence position s, compute the policy ratio, clipped surrogate, and reference-policy penalty:

\[ r_{b,s} = \exp\!\left(\text{log_pi}_{b,s} - \text{log_pi_old}_{b,s}\right) \] \[ L_{b,s}^{\mathrm{clip}} = \min\!\left( r_{b,s} A_{b,s}, \operatorname{clip}\!\left(r_{b,s}, 1 - \text{clip_eps}, 1 + \text{clip_eps}\right) A_{b,s} \right) \] \[ K_{b,s} = \text{log_ref}_{b,s} - \text{log_pi}_{b,s} \] \[ \text{output} = -\frac{1}{B S}\sum_{b=0}^{B-1}\sum_{s=0}^{S-1} \left(L_{b,s}^{\mathrm{clip}} - \text{beta}\,K_{b,s}\right) \]

Examples

For B = 1, S = 4, clip_eps = 0.2, and beta = 0.01:

\[ \text{advantages} = \begin{bmatrix} 1.0 & -2.0 & 3.0 & -4.0 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \text{log_pi} = \begin{bmatrix} \log(1.3) & \log(0.7) & \log(1.1) & \log(0.8) \end{bmatrix} \] \[ \text{log_pi_old} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.0 & 0.0 & 0.0 & 0.0 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \text{log_ref} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.1 & -0.2 & 0.05 & -0.1 \end{bmatrix} \] \[ r = \begin{bmatrix} 1.3 & 0.7 & 1.1 & 0.8 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \operatorname{clip}(r, 0.8, 1.2) = \begin{bmatrix} 1.2 & 0.8 & 1.1 & 0.8 \end{bmatrix} \] \[ L^{\mathrm{clip}} = \begin{bmatrix} 1.2 & -1.6 & 3.3 & -3.2 \end{bmatrix}, \quad K = \begin{bmatrix} -0.16236 & 0.15667 & -0.04531 & 0.12314 \end{bmatrix} \] \[ \text{output} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.07518 \end{bmatrix} \]

Constraints

  • 1 ≤ B ≤ 256
  • 1 ≤ S ≤ 16,384
  • All tensors contain 32-bit floating point values
  • 0 ≤ clip_eps < 1 and 0 ≤ beta ≤ 1
  • Policy log probabilities are finite, so the policy ratio is well-defined
  • Performance is measured with B = 128, S = 2,048
