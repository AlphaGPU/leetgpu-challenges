Given precomputed advantages and log probabilities from the current policy, previous policy, and reference policy, compute a scalar PPO-style clipped surrogate loss. The operation combines a clipped policy ratio with a non-negative reference-policy KL regularizer and reduces the result across a batch of sequences.

Implementation Requirements

  • Implement solve(advantages, log_pi, log_pi_old, log_ref, output, clip_eps, beta, B, S) with the signature unchanged
  • Write the single scalar result into the preallocated output tensor
  • Use only native features of the selected framework; external libraries are not permitted
  • All input tensors have shape [B, S] and the output has shape [1]
  • All S positions are valid response tokens; padding and token masks are outside the scope of this challenge
  • advantages, log_pi_old, and log_ref are fixed precomputed inputs; do not differentiate through them

For each batch element b and sequence position s, compute the policy ratio, clipped surrogate, and reference-policy penalty:

\[ \begin{aligned} r_{b,s} &= \exp\!\left( \text{log_pi}_{b,s} - \text{log_pi_old}_{b,s} \right), \\ L_{b,s}^{\mathrm{clip}} &= \min\!\Bigl( r_{b,s} A_{b,s}, \\ &\qquad \operatorname{clip}\!\left(r_{b,s}, 1 - \text{clip_eps}, 1 + \text{clip_eps}\right) A_{b,s} \Bigr), \\ d_{b,s} &= \text{log_ref}_{b,s} - \text{log_pi}_{b,s}, \\ K_{b,s} &= \exp(d_{b,s}) - d_{b,s} - 1, \\ \text{output} &= -\frac{1}{B S}\sum_{b=0}^{B-1}\sum_{s=0}^{S-1} \left(L_{b,s}^{\mathrm{clip}} - \text{beta}\,K_{b,s}\right). \end{aligned} \]

Examples

For B = 1, S = 4, clip_eps = 0.2, and beta = 0.01:

\[ \begin{aligned} \text{advantages} &= \begin{bmatrix} 1.0 & -2.0 & 3.0 & -4.0 \end{bmatrix}, \\ \text{log_pi} &= \begin{bmatrix} \log(1.3) & \log(0.7) & \log(1.1) & \log(0.8) \end{bmatrix}, \\ \text{log_pi_old} &= \begin{bmatrix} 0.0 & 0.0 & 0.0 & 0.0 \end{bmatrix}, \\ \text{log_ref} &= \begin{bmatrix} 0.1 & -0.2 & 0.05 & -0.1 \end{bmatrix}, \\ r &= \begin{bmatrix} 1.3 & 0.7 & 1.1 & 0.8 \end{bmatrix}, \\ \operatorname{clip}(r, 0.8, 1.2) &= \begin{bmatrix} 1.2 & 0.8 & 1.1 & 0.8 \end{bmatrix}, \\ L^{\mathrm{clip}} &= \begin{bmatrix} 1.2 & -1.6 & 3.3 & -3.2 \end{bmatrix}, \\ K &\approx \begin{bmatrix} 0.01250 & 0.01294 & 0.00101 & 0.00790 \end{bmatrix}. \end{aligned} \] \[ \text{output} \approx \begin{bmatrix} 0.07509 \end{bmatrix} \]

Constraints

  • 1 ≤ B ≤ 256
  • 1 ≤ S ≤ 16,384
  • All tensors contain 32-bit floating point values
  • 0 ≤ clip_eps < 1 and 0 ≤ beta ≤ 1
  • Both log_pi - log_pi_old and log_ref - log_pi lie in [-16, 16], so every exponential is finite in float32
  • Performance is measured with B = 128, S = 2,048

Reference

Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms, Schulman et al. (2017)
