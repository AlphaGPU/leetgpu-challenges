Given the log probabilities of chosen and rejected responses under the current policy and a reference policy, compute the Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) sequence loss. The sequence log probabilities have already been summed over response tokens, so this kernel operates on four one-dimensional tensors of shape [B] and produces one mean loss value.

Implementation Requirements

  • Implement solve(chosen_logps, rejected_logps, chosen_ref_logps, rejected_ref_logps, output, beta, B) with the signature unchanged
  • Write the scalar mean loss into the preallocated output tensor at output[0]
  • Use only native features of the selected framework; external libraries are not permitted
  • The input tensors contain sequence-level log probabilities of shape [B]
  • Compute the loss stably as softplus(-logits) or an equivalent stable formulation; do not rely on log(sigmoid(logits)) without numerical stabilization

Loss Calculation

For each sequence i, first compare the chosen and rejected responses under both policies:

\[ m_i = \text{chosen_logps}_i - \text{rejected_logps}_i \] \[ m_i^{\mathrm{ref}} = \text{chosen_ref_logps}_i - \text{rejected_ref_logps}_i \] \[ z_i = \beta \left(m_i - m_i^{\mathrm{ref}}\right) \]

The DPO loss is the negative log-sigmoid of z, averaged over the batch:

\[ \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{DPO}} = -\frac{1}{B}\sum_{i=0}^{B-1} \log\!\left(\sigma(z_i)\right) = \frac{1}{B}\sum_{i=0}^{B-1} \operatorname{softplus}(-z_i) \]

The softplus form is mathematically equivalent but remains finite for very large positive or negative logits.

Examples

For B = 4 and beta = 0.1:

\[ \text{chosen_logps} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.0 & 1.0 & -1.0 & 2.0 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \text{rejected_logps} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.0 & 0.0 & 0.0 & 0.0 \end{bmatrix} \] \[ \text{chosen_ref_logps} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.0 & 0.0 & 0.0 & 0.0 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \text{rejected_ref_logps} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.0 & 0.0 & 0.0 & 0.0 \end{bmatrix} \] \[ z = \begin{bmatrix} 0.0 & 0.1 & -0.1 & 0.2 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \text{output} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.67002 \end{bmatrix} \]

Constraints

  • 1 ≤ B ≤ 65,536
  • All input and output tensors contain 32-bit floating point values
  • 0 ≤ beta ≤ 1
  • All input log probabilities are finite
  • Sequence-level log probabilities are precomputed; token masks and sequence reductions are outside the scope of this challenge
  • Performance is measured with B = 65,536
