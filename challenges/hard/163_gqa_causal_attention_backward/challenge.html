Implement the backward pass of causal grouped-query attention (GQA), the attention used to train Llama, Mistral and Qwen models. The forward pass already ran and saved its output O and the per-row log-sum-exp lse of the attention scores, just as FlashAttention does. Given the upstream gradient dO, compute the gradients dQ, dK and dV. Several query heads share each key/value head, so the gradient of a shared key/value head is the sum of the contributions of all query heads in its group. All tensors are float32.

Implementation Requirements

Implement solve(Q, K, V, O, dO, lse, dQ, dK, dV, num_q_heads, num_kv_heads, seq_len, head_dim):

  • Q, O, dO: shape (num_q_heads, seq_len, head_dim)
  • K, V: shape (num_kv_heads, seq_len, head_dim)
  • lse: shape (num_q_heads, seq_len), the natural-log log-sum-exp of each row of scaled, masked scores saved by the forward pass
  • dQ: shape (num_q_heads, seq_len, head_dim); dK, dV: shape (num_kv_heads, seq_len, head_dim)

Query head h uses key/value head g = h / (num_q_heads / num_kv_heads) (integer division), so consecutive query heads share a key/value head. With \(\tau = 1/\sqrt{head\_dim}\), for query position i and key position j the forward pass used \[ s_{ij} = \tau\, Q_h[i] \cdot K_g[j], \qquad P_{ij} = \begin{cases} \exp(s_{ij} - lse_h[i]) & j \le i \\ 0 & j > i \end{cases}, \qquad O_h[i] = \sum_{j} P_{ij}\, V_g[j] \] (each token attends to itself and all earlier tokens). The gradients are \[ dP_{ij} = dO_h[i] \cdot V_g[j], \qquad D_i = dO_h[i] \cdot O_h[i], \qquad dS_{ij} = P_{ij}\,(dP_{ij} - D_i) \] \[ dQ_h[i] = \tau \sum_{j} dS_{ij}\, K_g[j], \qquad dK_g[j] = \tau \sum_{h \in g} \sum_{i} dS_{ij}\, Q_h[i], \qquad dV_g[j] = \sum_{h \in g} \sum_{i} P_{ij}\, dO_h[i] \] where \(h \in g\) ranges over the num_q_heads / num_kv_heads query heads that share key/value head g. (\(D_i\) equals \(\sum_j P_{ij} dP_{ij}\).)

  • Use only native features of the framework you choose (no external libraries); keep the function signature unchanged
  • Write the results into dQ, dK and dV; their initial contents are undefined, so every entry must be written. Do not modify the inputs
  • O and lse come from a float32 forward pass on the same Q, K, V; you may use them as given or recompute them
  • Accumulate dot products and sums in float32 or better; reduced-precision (TF32/FP16) products exceed the tolerance, even on the example. TF32 is the default for float32 matmuls with Triton tl.dot and with JAX on A100/H100/B200, so use input_precision="ieee" in Triton or precision=jax.lax.Precision.HIGHEST in JAX

Example

Input: num_q_heads = 2, num_kv_heads = 1, seq_len = 3, head_dim = 2 (both query heads share key/value head 0; O and lse shown rounded to 4 decimals): \[ Q_0 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad Q_1 = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \\ -1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad K_0 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad V_0 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \] \[ dO_0 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad dO_1 = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{bmatrix} \] \[ O_0 = \begin{bmatrix} 1.0000 & 2.0000 \\ 2.3395 & -0.0093 \\ 1.6044 & 0.5989 \end{bmatrix}, \quad O_1 = \begin{bmatrix} 1.0000 & 2.0000 \\ 1.6605 & 1.0093 \\ 2.3794 & -0.2890 \end{bmatrix}, \quad lse = \begin{bmatrix} 0.7071 & 1.1079 & 1.6206 \\ 0.0000 & 1.1079 & 1.0168 \end{bmatrix} \] Output (rounded to 4 decimals): \[ dQ_0 = \begin{bmatrix} 0.0000 & 0.0000 \\ 0.4692 & -0.4692 \\ -0.8493 & 1.1298 \end{bmatrix}, \quad dQ_1 = \begin{bmatrix} 0.0000 & 0.0000 \\ 0.1564 & -0.1564 \\ 0.3220 & -0.4699 \end{bmatrix} \] \[ dK_0 = \begin{bmatrix} -0.5864 & 0.0743 \\ 1.0149 & 0.0582 \\ -0.4284 & -0.1325 \end{bmatrix}, \quad dV_0 = \begin{bmatrix} 1.8925 & 1.5989 \\ -0.0023 & 0.5989 \\ 0.1098 & -0.1978 \end{bmatrix} \] Row 0 of each dQ is zero because token 0 attends only to itself (\(P_{00} = 1\), so \(dS_{00} = dP_{00} - D_0 = 0\)).

Constraints

  • All tensors are float32
  • 1 ≤ num_kv_heads ≤ num_q_heads ≤ 32, and num_q_heads is divisible by num_kv_heads
  • 1 ≤ seq_len ≤ 4,096; 1 ≤ head_dim ≤ 128
  • Scores \(s_{ij}\) can exceed 88, so \(\exp(s_{ij})\) on its own can overflow float32; \(\exp(s_{ij} - lse_h[i])\) is always ≤ 1
  • Performance is measured with num_q_heads = 32, num_kv_heads = 8, seq_len = 2,048, head_dim = 64
