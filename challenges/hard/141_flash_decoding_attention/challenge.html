Implement flash decoding attention: the long-context decode step of LLM inference (Flash-Decoding, Split-KV, Parallel Attention). A small batch of T queries (typically 1, the token being generated) attends to a KV cache of length S that is often tens of thousands of tokens. For each head h:

\[ s_{t,s} = \frac{Q_{t,h} \cdot K_{s,h}}{\sqrt{D}}, \qquad \text{output}_{t,h} = \sum_{s=0}^{S-1} \frac{e^{s_{t,s}}}{\sum_{s'=0}^{S-1} e^{s_{t,s'}}} \, V_{s,h} \]

Q has shape T × H × D, K and V have shape S × H × D, and output has shape T × H × D. All tensors are 32-bit floats. Unlike prefilling, T ≪ S, so a fast solution splits the KV cache across thread blocks, runs an online softmax per split and combines the partial outputs (flash decoding) rather than materializing a full T × S attention matrix.

Implementation Requirements

  • Do not change the function signature.
  • Do not use external libraries beyond what is available in the starter.
  • Write the result to output.
  • The softmax must be numerically stable for large scores.

Example

Input: T = 2, S = 3, H = 1, D = 2 \[ Q = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad K = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad V = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \\ 5 & 6 \end{bmatrix} \] Output (rounded): \[ \begin{bmatrix} 3.0000 & 4.0000 \\ 3.4067 & 4.4067 \end{bmatrix} \]

Constraints

  • 1 ≤ T ≤ 16
  • 1 ≤ S ≤ 131,072
  • 1 ≤ H ≤ 64
  • 1 ≤ D ≤ 256
  • Input values are in [-6, 6]
  • Performance is measured with T = 1, S = 65,536, H = 32, D = 128
