Compute the gradients of the mean cross-entropy loss of a language-model output head with respect to both its inputs, without materializing the full logits matrix. Given hidden states X of shape N × D, an output projection W of shape V × D (one row per vocabulary token) and integer targets targets of length N, where a target of -100 marks an ignored (padding) row, write \(\partial \text{loss} / \partial X\) to dX and \(\partial \text{loss} / \partial W\) to dW:

\[ z_{i,v} = \sum_{d} X_{i,d} W_{v,d}, \qquad \text{loss} = \frac{1}{n_{\text{valid}}} \sum_{i \,:\, \text{targets}_i \ne -100} \Big( \log \sum_{v=0}^{V-1} e^{z_{i,v}} - z_{i,\,\text{targets}_i} \Big) \] \[ G_{i,v} = \begin{cases} \dfrac{\text{softmax}(z_i)_v - [v = \text{targets}_i]}{n_{\text{valid}}} & \text{targets}_i \ne -100 \\ 0 & \text{targets}_i = -100 \end{cases}, \qquad dX = G\,W, \qquad dW = G^{\top} X \]

\(n_{\text{valid}}\) is the number of rows whose target is not -100. If every row is ignored, both gradients are all zeros. All tensors are 32-bit floats except targets, which is 32-bit integer. The N × V logits matrix is far larger than the inputs, so a fast solution recomputes logit tiles on the fly, fusing the matmul with an online log-sum-exp over vocabulary tiles and the two gradient matmuls — the backward half of the Liger / "cut cross-entropy" kernels.

Implementation Requirements

  • Do not change the function signature.
  • Do not use external libraries beyond what is available in the starter.
  • Write the gradients to dX and dW. Neither buffer is zero-initialized, so every entry must be written (rows of ignored targets, and vocabulary rows that no target points to, still receive their gradient or zero).
  • The computation must be numerically stable for logits of several hundred (subtract the row maximum).

Example 1:

Input (N = 3, D = 2, V = 3; row 2 is ignored, so \(n_{\text{valid}} = 2\)): \[ X = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad W = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \text{targets} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 2 & -100 \end{bmatrix} \] Logits of the valid rows are \(z_0 = [1, 0, 1]\) and \(z_1 = [0, 2, 2]\), so \[ G = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{e}{2e+1} - 1\right) & \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{2e+1} & \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{e}{2e+1} \\ \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{1+2e^2} & \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{e^2}{1+2e^2} & \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{e^2}{1+2e^2} - 1\right) \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix} \approx \begin{bmatrix} -0.2888406 & 0.0776812 & 0.2111594 \\ 0.0316895 & 0.2341553 & -0.2658447 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix} \] Output: \[ dX = \begin{bmatrix} -0.0776812 & 0.2888406 \\ -0.2341553 & -0.0316895 \\ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}, \quad dW = \begin{bmatrix} -0.2888406 & 0.0633789 \\ 0.0776812 & 0.4683105 \\ 0.2111594 & -0.5316895 \end{bmatrix} \]

Constraints

  • 1 ≤ N ≤ 8,192
  • 1 ≤ D ≤ 4,096
  • 2 ≤ V ≤ 65,536
  • targets[i] = -100 or 0 ≤ targets[i] < V
  • Logit magnitudes may reach several hundred
  • Performance is measured with N = 4,096, D = 1,024, V = 32,768
