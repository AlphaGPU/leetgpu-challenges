Implement the forward pass of multi-scale deformable attention, the core inference kernel of Deformable DETR and related detection / BEV vision transformers. Instead of attending to every spatial location, each query attends to only \(L \times P\) learned sampling points spread across \(L\) feature-map levels. Each sampling point lands at a fractional coordinate, so its feature vector must be reconstructed by bilinear interpolation and then combined using precomputed attention weights. All floating-point tensors are float32; spatial_shapes is int32.

The \(L\) feature maps are packed back-to-back along the spatial axis of value, which has shape \((N, S, H, D)\) with \(S = \sum_{\ell} h_\ell w_\ell\). Level \(\ell\) starts at row \(\text{start}_\ell = \sum_{j < \ell} h_j w_j\), and pixel \((x, y)\) of that level lives at row \(\text{start}_\ell + y\,w_\ell + x\). The per-level sizes are given by spatial_shapes, whose row \(\ell\) is \((h_\ell, w_\ell)\); the starting offsets are not provided.

For batch element \(n\), query \(q\), head \(h\), level \(\ell\) and point \(p\), let \((x, y) = \texttt{sampling_loc}[n, q, h, \ell, p]\) be a location normalized to \([0, 1]\) relative to level \(\ell\). Convert it to pixel coordinates and interpolate:

\[ \begin{aligned} p_x &= x\,w_\ell - 0.5, \qquad p_y = y\,h_\ell - 0.5 \\[4pt] x_0 &= \lfloor p_x \rfloor, \quad y_0 = \lfloor p_y \rfloor, \quad \delta_x = p_x - x_0, \quad \delta_y = p_y - y_0 \\[6pt] \text{Sample}_\ell(n, h, x, y) &= \sum_{c_y \in \{0,1\}} \sum_{c_x \in \{0,1\}} \omega_{c_x} \, \omega_{c_y} \cdot V_\ell\bigl[n,\, y_0 + c_y,\, x_0 + c_x,\, h\bigr] \\[4pt] &\quad \omega_{c_x} = \begin{cases} 1 - \delta_x & c_x = 0 \\ \delta_x & c_x = 1 \end{cases}, \qquad \omega_{c_y} = \begin{cases} 1 - \delta_y & c_y = 0 \\ \delta_y & c_y = 1 \end{cases} \end{aligned} \]

Corners that fall outside the level (\(x_0 + c_x \notin [0, w_\ell - 1]\) or \(y_0 + c_y \notin [0, h_\ell - 1]\)) contribute zero — the feature map is zero-padded, never clamped. The output is the attention-weighted sum over all levels and points:

\[ \texttt{output}[n, q, h\,D + d] = \sum_{\ell=0}^{L-1} \sum_{p=0}^{P-1} \texttt{attn_weight}[n, q, h, \ell, p] \cdot \text{Sample}_\ell\bigl(n, h, \texttt{sampling_loc}[n, q, h, \ell, p]\bigr)_d \]

Implementation Requirements

  • Use only native features (external libraries are not permitted)
  • The solve function signature must remain unchanged
  • The final result must be stored in the output tensor of shape \((N, Q, H D)\)
  • Compute the level starting offsets yourself from spatial_shapes
  • All tensors are contiguous and row-major: value[n, s, h, d] sits at offset ((n*S + s)*H + h)*D + d, and sampling_loc[n, q, h, l, p, i] at ((((n*Q + q)*H + h)*L + l)*P + p)*2 + i
  • Sampling locations are not guaranteed to lie inside \([0, 1]\); out-of-range corners must be treated as zeros
  • The last axis of sampling_loc is ordered \((x, y)\) — column first, row second

Example

Input: \(N = 1,\; Q = 1,\; H = 1,\; D = 2,\; L = 2,\; P = 1,\; S = 5\), with \(\texttt{spatial_shapes} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 2 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}\) (a \(2 \times 2\) level followed by a \(1 \times 1\) level).

The five packed feature rows of value (rows 0–3 are level 0 in row-major order, row 4 is level 1): \[ \texttt{value}[0, :, 0, :] = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \\ 5 & 6 \\ 7 & 8 \\ 10 & 20 \end{bmatrix} \]

Both sampling points sit at the center of their level, and the attention weights favor level 0: \[ \texttt{sampling_loc}[0, 0, 0, :, 0, :] = \begin{bmatrix} 0.5 & 0.5 \\ 0.5 & 0.5 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \texttt{attn_weight}[0, 0, 0, :, :] = \begin{bmatrix} 0.75 \\ 0.25 \end{bmatrix} \]

Level 0: \(p_x = 0.5 \cdot 2 - 0.5 = 0.5\) and \(p_y = 0.5\), so \(x_0 = y_0 = 0\) and \(\delta_x = \delta_y = 0.5\) — all four pixels get weight \(0.25\), giving \(\tfrac{1}{4}\bigl([1, 2] + [3, 4] + [5, 6] + [7, 8]\bigr) = [4, 5]\).

Level 1: \(p_x = 0.5 \cdot 1 - 0.5 = 0\) and \(p_y = 0\), so the point lands exactly on the single pixel, giving \([10, 20]\).

Output: \[ \texttt{output}[0, 0, :] = 0.75 \cdot [4, 5] + 0.25 \cdot [10, 20] = \begin{bmatrix} 5.5 & 8.75 \end{bmatrix} \]

Constraints

  • 1 ≤ N ≤ 8
  • 1 ≤ Q ≤ 30,000
  • 1 ≤ H ≤ 16
  • 1 ≤ D ≤ 64
  • 1 ≤ L ≤ 8,   1 ≤ P ≤ 16
  • 1 ≤ \(h_\ell\), \(w_\ell\) ≤ 256 and S = \(\sum_\ell h_\ell w_\ell\)
  • attn_weight[n, q, h, :, :] is non-negative and sums to 1
  • spatial_shapes is int32; all other tensors are float32
  • Performance is measured with N = 2, Q = 20,000, H = 8, D = 32, L = 4, P = 4 and levels 128 × 128, 64 × 64, 32 × 32, 16 × 16 (S = 21,760)
