Implement the expert computation of a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) feed-forward layer, as used in Mixtral, DeepSeek and Qwen-MoE. Each of the T tokens in x (T × H) has already been routed to K distinct experts: expert indices are in topk_ids (T × K, 32-bit int) and combine weights in topk_weights (T × K). Every expert e is a SwiGLU MLP with weights w_gate[e], w_up[e] (H × I) and w_down[e] (I × H). Compute

\[ \text{output}_t = \sum_{k=0}^{K-1} \text{topk_weights}_{t,k}\, \Big( \text{SiLU}(x_t W^{gate}_{e}) \odot (x_t W^{up}_{e}) \Big) W^{down}_{e}, \qquad e = \text{topk_ids}_{t,k} \]

where \(\text{SiLU}(z) = z / (1 + e^{-z})\). All floating-point tensors are 32-bit. A fast solution groups tokens by expert and fuses gate/up GEMMs, the SwiGLU activation, the down projection and the weighted scatter-combine instead of running a dense layer per expert.

Implementation Requirements

  • Do not change the function signature.
  • Do not use external libraries beyond what is available in the starter.
  • Write the result to output.
  • Use topk_weights exactly as given (they are not guaranteed to be normalized or positive).
  • The K expert ids of a token are distinct; some experts may receive no tokens.

Example 1:

Input: T = 2, H = 2, I = 2, E = 2, K = 1 \[ x = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \text{topk_ids} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \text{topk_weights} = \begin{bmatrix} 1.0 \\ 0.5 \end{bmatrix} \] \[ W^{gate}_0 = W^{gate}_1 = W^{down}_0 = W^{down}_1 = W^{up}_0 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad W^{up}_1 = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \] Output: \[ \text{output} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.7311 & 0 \\ 0 & 0.7311 \end{bmatrix} \] Token 0: \(\text{SiLU}(1) \cdot 1 = 0.7311\). Token 1: \(0.5 \cdot \text{SiLU}(1) \cdot 2 = 0.7311\).

Constraints

  • 1 ≤ T ≤ 8,192
  • 1 ≤ H, I ≤ 4,096
  • 1 ≤ K ≤ E ≤ 64
  • 0 ≤ topk_ids[t][k] < E, distinct within a row
  • Performance is measured with T = 4,096, H = 1,024, I = 1,408, E = 16, K = 2
