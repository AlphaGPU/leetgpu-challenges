Implement Kimi Delta Attention (KDA), the linear attention mechanism introduced in "Kimi Linear: An Expressive, Efficient Attention Architecture". KDA extends the gated delta rule with a channel-wise forget gate: the recurrent state \( S_t \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d} \) is a fast-weight associative memory that is decayed per feature channel, corrected by the delta rule, and then queried. For each batch, the state starts at \( S_0 = 0 \) and is updated for t = 1, ..., T: $$ S_t = \left(I - \beta_t k_t k_t^\top\right) \text{Diag}(\alpha_t)\, S_{t-1} + \beta_t k_t v_t^\top $$ $$ o_t = S_t^\top q_t $$ where \( q_t, k_t, v_t \in \mathbb{R}^{d} \) are the query, key, and value vectors at step t, \( \alpha_t \in (0, 1]^{d} \) is the channel-wise decay gate, and \( \beta_t \in [0, 1) \) is the update strength. The update can be read as online gradient descent on the reconstruction loss \( \frac{1}{2}\lVert S^\top k_t - v_t \rVert^2 \) with per-channel weight decay: old associations fade channel by channel while the delta rule overwrites the value currently associated with \( k_t \).

All tensors are float32. Q, K, V, alpha, and output have shape batch × T × d; beta has shape batch × T. Key vectors are L2-normalized, so \( \lVert k_t \rVert_2 = 1 \) (or \( k_t = 0 \)). Each batch element is an independent sequence.

Implementation Requirements

  • Use only native features (external libraries are not permitted)
  • The solve function signature must remain unchanged
  • The final result must be stored in output

Example

Input (batch = 1, T = 2, d = 2): $$ Q = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix},\quad K = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix},\quad V = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 0 \end{bmatrix},\quad \alpha = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0.5 & 0.5 \end{bmatrix},\quad \beta = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0.5 \end{bmatrix} $$

Step 1: \( S_1 = k_1 v_1^\top = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix} \), so \( o_1 = S_1^\top q_1 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix} \). Step 2: the decayed state is \( \begin{bmatrix} 0.5 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix} \), and the delta update with \( k_2 = (0, 1) \) adds \( 0.5\, k_2 v_2^\top \), giving \( S_2 = \begin{bmatrix} 0.5 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix} \) and \( o_2 = S_2^\top q_2 = \begin{bmatrix} 1.5 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} \).

Output: $$ \text{output} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 1.5 & 1 \end{bmatrix} $$

Constraints

  • 1 ≤ batch ≤ 8
  • 1 ≤ T ≤ 16,384
  • 1 ≤ d ≤ 128
  • 0 < alpha values ≤ 1, 0 ≤ beta values < 1
  • Rows of K are L2-normalized (unit norm or all-zero)
  • Performance is measured with batch = 4, T = 8,192, d = 128
